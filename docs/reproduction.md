# Detailed reproduction notes / Microsoft

An independent corporate financial forecasting and performance analysis case study, implemented from the attached business case. It asks: **under which operating assumptions can growth generate durable free cash flow, and which cash drivers should management prioritize?**

## Open first

- `models/financial_model.xlsx`: editable driver assumptions, five reported annual periods, three integrated projected statements for each scenario, sensitivity tables, raw-fact references and live balance checks.
- `docs/executive_memo.pdf`: two-page management recommendation.
- `dashboards/financial_report.pdf`: four accessible report pages.
- `docs/executive_walkthrough.pdf`: seven-slide, 3–5 minute presentation.
- `dashboards/web/index.html`: interactive dashboard (serve over HTTP as below).

## Scope and evidence

Microsoft Corporation (MSFT), CIK 0000789019. Fiscal year ends June 30. FY2022–2026 actuals, FY2027–2029 forecast. FY2019–2021 retained for historical warmup. Evidence snapshot: **November 7, 2026**. Monetary source facts are USD divided by 1,000,000; the browser and reports display USD billions. No interim FY2027 information is used. This is a deliberately annual-only case, not a forecast incorporating every disclosure available on the snapshot date.

Every historical field has a standard XBRL tag, fiscal dates, original filing date, accession, unit and source URL. `results/filing_reconciliation.csv` compares 165 selected annual facts to separately archived original 10-K inline XBRL. All pass. The normalized history uses each annual fact's earliest available 10-K, rather than subsequently restated comparators. Original reporting policy may cause differences versus later restated series; no quantitative restatement impact study is included.

## Results from this implementation

- FY2026 actual revenue: $331.8B; EBIT: $155.2B; cash capex: $115.9B; FCF: $67.0B.
- FY2029 base revenue: $522.8B; FCF: $104.3B. Defined downside/upside FCF: $75.4B–$129.7B.
- Four one-year holdouts, FY2023–2026. MAPE: revenue 4.6%, EBIT 8.0%, FCF 18.4%.
- Relative to a prior-year metric-growth baseline, MAE is 12.0% lower for revenue, 18.6% lower for FCF and **4.8% higher for EBIT**. Every FCF holdout overforecasts actual.
- 48 model gates pass, plus six Python test groups including a stress sweep and an independent evaluator for all workbook formulas. Implementation checks do not validate future assumptions.

No claim of improved company profitability, causal savings, company sponsorship, statistical significance or live forecasting performance is made. Educational analysis; not investment advice.

## Source refresh

SEC requires a descriptive User-Agent identifying the requester. Set `SEC_USER_AGENT` to your own research identity and contact email before requesting new data; do not send a fabricated identity. Stay within SEC fair-access limits. One sequential fetch is used at a time.

```sh
export SEC_USER_AGENT='Your Research Name your-contact@example.com'
python src/ingest.py --refresh
python src/reconcile.py
python src/build.py
```

Refresh preserves the previous raw API response under a hash-named archive before replacing the working snapshot. The as-of policy and year range are fixed in code, so a refresh does not silently extend the model horizon. Extend `ingest.py`, the fixed backtest protocol and reports deliberately before adding another fiscal year. The refreshed SEC response can change historical availability or restatements; compare against the archived raw snapshot. `docs/raw_sha256.txt` records the working snapshot hash. Reconciliation fetches missing HTML only; archived original filings are reused.

## Modeling logic

Base revenue growth is the mean of the last two annual growth rates. Operating and gross margins, effective tax rate and SBC ratio use three annual observations. Latest-year ratios anchor collections, inventory, payables, deferred revenue, capex, leases, depreciation, amortization, interest and payouts. `docs/assumptions_register.csv` records all 18 drivers, units, owner, date, basis and scenario ranges. No segment forecast is implied.

Revenue and margins drive earnings. Net income feeds CFO and equity; SBC is added to both CFO and equity. Operating balance changes feed CFO. Cash capex and noncash finance lease additions feed PP&E, reduced by depreciation. Intangible amortization reduces intangible assets. Lease additions and principal payments roll lease debt forward. FCF = CFO minus **cash** capex. Lease principal, dividends and buybacks flow through financing cash flow. Cash is a cash-flow result.

The minimum cash floor equals FY2026 cash. A disclosed new-borrowing rule covers shortfalls; it is not an unexplained balance-sheet plug. Interest uses opening long-term debt plus finance leases, avoiding circularity. Existing long-term principal is held constant, and this financing proxy does not model actual maturities or issuance spreads. Other nonoperating income is normalized to zero rather than extrapolating FY2026 gains.

Upside shocks: +3 pp growth, +2 pp operating margin, −3 collection days, −2 pp cash capex intensity. Downside: −5 pp growth, −3 pp operating margin, +5 collection days, +2 pp capex. These are analyst judgments. All assumptions are conditional and adjustable in Excel; four primary drivers are adjustable in the browser. The sensitivity grids are anchored to the original base, independently of custom browser changes.

## Backtest protocol and limitations

Expanding windows use original annual facts available at FY2022–2025 filing-date origins; targets are FY2023–2026. The forecasting method is the same as the current model. Historical origin data must have at least three prior annual observations. The naive baseline applies each metric's latest annual growth to its last actual. No parameter was optimized on the holdout errors during implementation. The retrospective analyst still observes historical outcomes, so researcher hindsight remains possible.

MAE is mean absolute error in USD millions; MAPE is mean absolute error divided by absolute actual; WAPE divides total absolute error by total absolute actual; bias is mean(forecast − actual). Baseline gap is baseline MAE − model MAE. Four origins are too few for a robust skill claim. All evaluated actuals are positive and nonzero; MAPE remains sensitive to low FCF values.

Annual working-capital ratios use end-of-year balances. Payables can include construction-related obligations. Depreciation uses latest depreciation / ending PP&E applied to future opening PP&E; this is not an asset cohort schedule. Interest uses a blended financing proxy. Other assets (including marketable securities and goodwill) and other liabilities are fixed. No acquisitions, securities sales, new operating leases, detailed deferred taxes, FX forecast or debt maturity schedule. A balanced forecast demonstrates accounting linkage, not economic accuracy.

## Layout

## Three-minute reviewer walkthrough

1. Open the dashboard snapshot; distinguish actual and forecast values.
2. Open Drivers; explain capital intensity, margin and collections.
3. Change a scenario and one slider; inspect cash and borrowing, then open all three statements.
4. Open Accuracy; show the EBIT miss and FCF overforecast bias.
5. Open Sources; trace one raw fact to its original filing and download the workbook/memo.
